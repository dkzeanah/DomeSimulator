## 4. The stem cell (what you're actually buying)

- Image S1 (bare frame) + S2 (forty bays, one decision).
- The frame: 19.42 ft across, 277 sq ft of floor, forty triangular bays,
  120 wedge struts cut from about 1.5 of the buyer's own trees (664 ft of
  stock) — the shop ships hardware and hose, not wood.
- The core: the utility column that moves to the next dome. Image from
  `stem-cell-utility-core-build.mp4` stills.

## 5. It stacks hats (the v6 centrepiece)

- Images H2 (explosion), H3 (the growth), Q1–Q3 (the quilt story).
- The standard article wears a **shower cap**: outer wood panels, one
  monolithic membrane, quilted layers of recycled clothing at **$50 a
  layer**, and a rain-slick cap strapped over the lot.
- The hard laminated hull becomes the **upgrade** — same frame, whenever.
- The numbers: cap stack with three quilts **$3,471** against the hull
  and its bays at **$9,905** — **$6,434 saved**, and the cap keeps
  growing where the hull's cavity fills at four layers.

## 6. The mast, the floor, the floating dome

- Images M1–M2, F1–F2.
- The floor is the upgrade bought after the dome: steel hub clamps the
  mast, ten spokes, wooden deck — **$1,899**. The mast through the
  column: **$622**. The rig that hangs the whole thing between trees:
  **$1,490**. All three: **$4,011**.
- Label it honestly on the page: *design possibility — the loads are the
  engineer's number.*

## 7. The price story

- Image G1 + the live numbers table (README.md).
- Standard article: **$18,522 list**, $12,040 to build, $66.87 per sq ft.
  The hull version: **$27,581**. The host's pad: **$6,240** — and the
  dome's price carries no floor, no deck, no groundwork, on purpose.
- The floor price with every saving lever pulled: **$15,599**.
