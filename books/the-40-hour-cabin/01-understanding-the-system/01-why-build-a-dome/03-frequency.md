# Frequency

Frequency is how many times the original triangle has been divided.

A geodesic dome starts as an icosahedron: twenty identical triangles wrapped
round a ball. That is frequency one. Divide every edge in two and push the new
points out to the sphere, and you have frequency two. Divide in three, and you
have frequency three.

Each division makes the dome rounder and makes everything about it smaller and
more numerous.

WHAT IT COSTS TO GO UP ONE

    frequency   struts   lengths   panels   vertices
    --------------------------------------------------
    1V              25         1       15         11
    2V              65         2       40         26   <- this book
    3V             165         5      105         61
    4V             250         6      160         91

Those are counted by building each one, at the same radius, rather than read
off a table.

WHY THIS BOOK IS 2V

Three reasons, and only the first is about the shape.

**Two lengths.** A 2V dome has 2 strut lengths. A 3V has
5. Every extra length is another saw setting, another
batch to keep separate, another chance to reach for the wrong stick on the
forty-first panel.

**A number of parts one person can handle.** 65 geodesic edges
becomes 120 members once the pinwheel joint
duplicates each edge into the two panels that share it. At 3V that would be
165 edges and 304 members, which is
a different kind of project.

**The member is the right size.** At the reference build's diameter a 2V long
member is 72.000 inches -- a six-foot stick, which is
what comes out of a twelve-foot log section, what one person carries, and
what fits in a pickup. Going to 3V at the same diameter makes every member
about three feet, which wastes the length a tree gives you for free.

WHEN TO GO UP

Above about twenty feet across. The 2V's flat panels get large enough to
start carrying bending they should not, the faceting becomes obvious, and the
member length passes comfortable one-person handling. Chapter 19 says where
that line is and what this book does and does not know about it.

WHAT FREQUENCY IS NOT

It is not a measure of quality. A 4V dome is not a better building than a 2V
one; it is a rounder one with 185 more struts and
4 more lengths to keep track of.
Pick the frequency that gives you a member you can make and carry, and let
the shape follow.
