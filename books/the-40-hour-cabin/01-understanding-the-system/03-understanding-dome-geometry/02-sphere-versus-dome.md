# Sphere versus dome

A geodesic sphere is a closed ball of triangles. A geodesic dome is the part
of one you kept.

That is the whole distinction, and it matters more than it sounds.

The reference build is a hemisphere: exactly half a sphere, cut on the
equator. It sits on the ground on ten flat feet and the cut is level all the
way round.

WHY A HEMISPHERE AND NOT SOMETHING ELSE

You can cut a 2V sphere at the equator and you get a clean edge. Ten base
vertices, all at the same height, all level. Nothing has to be shimmed and
nothing is left hanging.

Cut it anywhere else and you do not get that. A three-eighths dome or a
five-eighths dome means cutting through triangles rather than between them,
and every one of those cuts is a new angle, a new length, and a new
opportunity to be wrong.

The hemisphere is the cut the geometry offers you for free. The reference
build takes it.

WHAT YOU GIVE UP

Honesty first: a hemisphere is not the most useful shape for a house.

The wall at the bottom is already leaning in. Stand at the edge of a
nineteen-foot dome and the ceiling is at your shoulder. The floor area says
277 square feet, but the floor area you can stand upright on is considerably
less than that, and a wardrobe against the wall does not fit against the wall.

A taller cut -- five-eighths of a sphere rather than a half -- gives you a
straighter wall and more usable room. It also gives you a base ring that is no
longer level, a set of triangles that are no longer the two families you know,
and a lot of new arithmetic.

The reference build chooses the easy cut and pays for it in headroom near the
edge. If you want the other trade, the geometry will do it. You will just be
solving a different dome.

THE FLOOR IS NOT A CIRCLE

One more thing that catches people.

The base of a 2V hemisphere is not round. It is a ten-sided polygon, because
there are ten base vertices and straight members between them.

The circle that would pass through those ten corners encloses 296 square feet.
The decagon actually enclosed by the members encloses 277.

Nineteen square feet of difference. Not enough to matter for most things, and
exactly enough to matter if you are buying flooring or pouring a slab and you
measured the circle.
