# Cut worksheets

One page, filled in before any wood is touched.

THE SETTINGS

    bevel, every member  22.5 deg
    mitre, setting 1  21.138 deg
    mitre, setting 2  30.000 deg
    mitre, setting 3  34.431 deg

3 settings for 120 members. The
bevel is constant because it comes from how the log was split; set it on the
first stick and do not touch it again.

THE CUT LIST, REFERENCE BUILD

    A members    60  at   70.182 in
    B members    60  at   62.681 in
    total   120       664 ft of stock

Add the waste fraction before buying or felling anything.

THE BLANK LENGTH

Longer than the cut, on the butt end, because the compound cut runs
2.23 to 2.91 inches along
the stick -- the sector's point reaches further than its bark corner.

Add three inches to every blank and trim to the line. It is cheaper than
finding out on the fortieth stick.

THE ORDER

Cut all the A members. Check three of them against each other before cutting
the rest. Then all the B.

Do not alternate. Every change of setting is a chance to get it wrong, and
3 settings done once is a different job from
3 settings done a hundred and twenty times.

THE FIRST STICK

Cut one. Cut a second. Put them together as the joint will hold them, and
look at the gap.

If there is one, the setting is wrong and you have wasted two sticks. If
there is not, cut the other hundred and eighteen.
