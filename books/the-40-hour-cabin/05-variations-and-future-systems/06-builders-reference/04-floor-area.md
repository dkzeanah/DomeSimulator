# Floor area

Two floor numbers, and the difference between them catches people out.

THE CIRCLE AND THE RING

The FLOOR CIRCLE is the full circle at the dome's widest point. It is what
you get if you multiply pi by the radius squared, and it is the number most
dome literature quotes.

The FLOOR RING is the ten-sided figure the base ring actually makes. It is
what the building encloses, and at every diameter it is about
6 percent
smaller.

Quote the second one. The first is a circle nobody built.

THE THIRD NUMBER NOBODY PUBLISHES

Usable floor -- the part with headroom.

A hemisphere's surface is 45 degrees from vertical at half its height, so
standing room shrinks fast toward the perimeter. At the reference build's
19.42 feet, the circle where the shell is six feet up is
considerably smaller than the base.

There is no table for this because it depends on what you call headroom.
Work it out for your own height before you decide a diameter, and work it out
again if you are considering a knee wall -- which moves the whole curve up
and is the single biggest change available to this number.
