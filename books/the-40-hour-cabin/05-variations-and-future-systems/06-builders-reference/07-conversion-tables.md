# Conversion tables

The handful of conversions this method actually needs.

LENGTH

    1 inch  25.4 mm exactly
    1 foot  304.8 mm exactly
    1 metre  39.3701 in

The reference build's radius is 116.4984 inches, which is
2.9591 metres. The simulator works in metres and
the tables in inches, and that is the conversion between them.

ANGLE

    the log sector  45 degrees
    the butt bevel  22.5 degrees (half the sector)
    A seam fold / gap  18.029 / 26.971 degrees
    B seam fold / gap  22.459 / 22.541 degrees

Both seam pairs sum to 45. That is not a
coincidence and it is the closest thing this method has to a theorem: the
sector you split out of the tree is exactly the seam budget the dome needs.

TIMBER

    1 board foot  144 cubic inches
    a 12-ft 2x6  12 board feet nominal

AREA AND VOLUME

    1 square metre  10.7639 sq ft
    1 cubic foot  7.4805 US gallons

That last one is for tanks. The reference build's under-floor water tank is
120 gallons, which is
16.0 cubic feet of space to find.
