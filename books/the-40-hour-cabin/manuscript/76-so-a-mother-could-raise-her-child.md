And the real one, the one that is true without a joke attached: I like taking

## Why women specifically

<!-- concept: hype3/whichones -->
<!-- concept: hype3/whywomen -->
<!-- concept: hype3/howihandled -->
<!-- concept: hype3/lunches -->

In Chapter {{ch.why_me}} I said that if there are people with the means to
fund something like this, women especially, they are who I would most like to
reach. I do not care which women: saints, sinners, anybody. (For what it is
worth, this structure would make a very cheap livestreaming studio.)

Why women specifically? Because my mother is an angel and a saint, and growing
up with a single parent was hard. We had a trailer that had to be repossessed.
She had to file for bankruptcy.

How did I handle it? As a teenager I avoided talking to girls, because I knew I
could not in good conscience afford to take anybody out.

And the part nobody says out loud: we were so poor that I did not take the
reduced school lunches, because of how absolutely death-blowingly embarrassing
that would have been. Getting it is not embarrassing. Getting it and *needing*
it is. And a lot of people need it.

## And then I heard Fuller

<!-- concept: hype3/fuller -->
<!-- concept: hype3/hooked -->

Buckminster Fuller, who gave us the geodesic dome and a great deal else, once
described his goal as housing affordable enough that a mother was not so
daily and endlessly burdened by it that she could not attend to the thing that
actually matters, which is raising her child. That is not a direct quotation.
It is what I took from him.

When I heard it, I knew immediately that this was the thing I had been
preparing to make my life's work. Hooked, determined, sold, without needing any
further proof. That is what I thought then and it is what I think now. It is
enormously larger than me, for all the right reasons, and it makes profound
sense on the mathematics -- which is the other half of this book.

## The proof already exists

<!-- concept: hype3/igloo -->

And if you want proof that the shape works, it already shipped. The Inuit
built compression domes out of the only material they had and lived in them
through some of the harshest conditions on the planet. What further proof do
you need? Ask me, and I will try to give it to you -- with a model you can run,
which is what the rest of this book is.

:::box clock "What the clock is really for"
The {{clock.week}}-hour clock in this book is not a speed record. It is the
answer to a single question: how much of a working week does a roof cost, if
the roof is built like this? For a single parent, the difference between a
week and a mortgage is the difference Fuller was talking about.
:::
