This rung is a story told in twelve short chapters, one for each idea in the
book. Read one a night. Every chapter is the whole of its idea at this size --
nothing is held back for later, it is only told simply -- so a child who hears
all twelve has heard the entire method, the same one the two volumes at the
back of this book take hundreds of pages to work through.

Each chapter has three parts, and they are the three reads this whole book is
built on:

* **the story** -- read it aloud as it is;
* **Try it together** -- something to do with your hands, from things already
  in the house;
* **For the grown-up** -- a few sentences of why, with the real numbers, so the
  person reading can answer the questions that come.

A **wonder question** closes each chapter. There is no wrong answer to it.
The best ones get written down: they are usually the questions the next rung
answers.
