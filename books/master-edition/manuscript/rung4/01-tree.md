The trees come down: felled with a planned notch and hinge, limbed, and bucked
into sections of one length, cutting out the bends and the flare. This is the
most dangerous work in the whole build, and the books do not soften it.

:::box do "Before you start"
Your safety plan from rung three is signed. The fall line of every tree is
chosen from its lean, the wind and the landing ground, and an escape route is
cleared for each. The second person knows their job is to watch the crown and
stay out of the fall. Your section length is decided and cut into a story pole.
:::

:::box done "Done when"
Every section is cut, counted twice, stacked off the ground, and the count
matches the struts your plan needs plus spares.
:::

:::box check "For checkers"
Read the felling and limbing procedures in Volume B against your own training. Is
every instruction about the notch, the hinge, the back cut and a log under tension
correct for the cases a first-time feller will meet?
:::
