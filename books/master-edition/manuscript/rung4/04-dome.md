Size the dome for real: from the dome you want, from the tree you have, or both
-- then run the round trip that proves the two calculations agree before
anything is cut.

:::box do "Before you start"
Rung three's worksheets one and four, filled in, with the convention -- chord or
cut member -- written at the top.
:::

:::box done "Done when"
Your radius, your longest member and your tree count agree in both directions,
and you know which of the books' two domes yours is closer to.
:::

:::box check "For checkers"
The two volumes size their domes from "six feet" in two different ways and get
two different domes. Read the comparison page at the back of this book. Is the
difference explained clearly enough that a builder could not mix the two up?
:::
