Build the jig, then run it: forty panels in two shapes, each located against the
same stops, screwed, flush-cut, checked against its drawing and stacked.

:::box do "Before you start"
A flat bench, set up for the first shape. The marking scheme decided. The panel
drawings printed for checking. Spares counted for the practice panels.
:::

:::box done "Done when"
Forty panels are stacked by shape, each one checked against its drawing, and
the jig's practice panels are kept as test pieces.
:::

:::box check "For checkers"
The jig chapters say "one bench, forty panels" while the dome has two panel
shapes. Is it clear how one bench builds both, and how the short-sided panels'
left and right sides are kept from being swapped?
:::
