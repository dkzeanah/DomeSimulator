From here up, this book stops retelling and starts routing. The full build is
already written -- twice, in the two volumes bound in behind these rungs -- and
the honest thing is to send you to it in the right order rather than summarise
it a third time.

So each of the twelve threads on this rung is a **card**. A card says what the
thread becomes when the work is real, what to have ready before you start, and
when you are done -- then gives the chapters of Volume A and Volume B to read, in
order, with their page numbers.

Volume A is the Complete Edition: the owner's own dome, computed end to end.
Volume B is the paperback: the same method told as a fortnight, from a measured
tree. Where they differ, the comparison page at the back says how and why.

Every card ends with a question **for checkers** -- the thing the author most
wants a second pair of eyes on. If you are reading this copy to check the work,
those questions are where to start.
