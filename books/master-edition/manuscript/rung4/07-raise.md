Raise it: the bottom ring set and checked, each course keyed and screwed as it
goes, props under every leaning panel until the top closes, and then the props
out.

:::box do "Before you start"
Rung three's worksheet seven: the method chosen, the crew named, the props cut,
the weather watched, and the place nobody stands while anything is lifted.
:::

:::box done "Done when"
The frame stands closed with no props, and the ring measures round across every
opposite pair of corners.
:::

:::box check "For checkers"
If the dome is to be hung from trees, the rigging, the trees and the winch carry
real loads. Do the volumes say clearly enough that those loads are an engineer's
and an arborist's to rate, and that nobody stands under a hanging load?
:::
