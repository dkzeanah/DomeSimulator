Log every hour and every receipt while you build -- not afterwards. The books'
own counts are partly estimates; a real build's log is the measurement that
turns them into facts.

:::box do "Before you start"
A notebook at the work, and the stages from rung three's worksheet eleven written
at the top of its pages.
:::

:::box done "Done when"
Your build has a log with every stage's hours and every receipt, each one
labelled measured.
:::

:::box check "For checkers"
The two volumes count their forty hours differently, and both report whole-build
hours elsewhere. Read the comparison page and the hours chapters together. Could
a reader come away with the wrong idea of how long the whole build takes?
:::
