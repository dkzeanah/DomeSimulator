**The open question -- the most important one in this book:** what does a skinned
dome actually do through a winter? How warm, how dry, how much heat does it take,
and does the moisture plan work?

:::box do "An experiment you can run"
Log a winter. Temperature and humidity outside, in the room, and at every layer of
the skin; the heat put in each day; every quilt layer added and when. Note every
morning of condensation and where it was. The project's own costed plan names this
exact measurement as its first priority, and promises to publish it whichever way
it comes out.
:::
