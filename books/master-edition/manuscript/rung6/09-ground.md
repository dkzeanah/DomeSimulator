**The open question:** could a network of serviced pads let a family own a building
without owning land -- and would a used dome hold its value when it moves?

:::box do "An experiment you can run"
Survey your area: who has a level, serviced piece of ground they would rent to a
dome? What would they charge? What would your local building department say about
a movable dwelling on it? Write down what you find and where the books' assumptions
were right or wrong.
:::

:::box check "For checkers"
The network is the least-built idea in the project. Is it presented as a proposal
everywhere it appears?
:::
