**The open question:** what is a tree on *your* land actually worth to *you*, on
each rung of the value ladder -- standing, as firewood, sawn, split, as a
building?

:::box do "An experiment you can run"
Pick one tree you could fell. Measure it as on rung two. Weigh one green section
(a bathroom scale and some arithmetic will do), split it, and weigh the wedges as
they dry over a month. Price the same volume as firewood and as lumber at your
local yard. Write down every number with its source.
:::

:::box check "For checkers"
The value ladder multiplies a tree's stump price many times over. Is it clear on
every rung that a substitution value is not income?
:::
