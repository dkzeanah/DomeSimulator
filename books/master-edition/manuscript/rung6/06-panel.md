**The open question:** how far can software carry a hand-built method -- numbering
every piece, drawing every cut, rendering every picture -- and where must a person
still decide?

:::box do "An experiment you can run"
Number every member of your build as the every-piece chapter does, mark the
numbers on the wood, and record where each one ended up in the frame. Afterwards,
compare the software's plan with what actually happened, and list every place they
differed and why.
:::

:::box check "For checkers"
Is it clear to a non-programmer what the software does, what it needs, and that a
build does not require running it?
:::
