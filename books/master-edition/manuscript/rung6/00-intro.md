The top rung is the frontier: the questions this project has computed and not yet
measured, and the ones nobody has asked yet. It is the rung where a family, a
class or a builder stops learning from the books and starts adding to them.

Each card names an **open question**, describes **an experiment you can run** --
what to build, what to measure, how to record it -- and routes you to the chapters
that set the question up. The rule for every experiment is the one the whole
project runs on: say what you measured, how, and what it showed, and publish the
result whichever way it came out. A result that does not help the argument is
worth more than one that does, because nobody else will print it.

Two experiments matter more than all the rest put together, and both are named
here: **log a real winter** in a skinned dome, and **publish a real build's hours
and receipts**. Every estimate in both volumes is waiting for one of them.
