**The open question:** can a whole dome be built and lived in up in the trees --
the web, the winch, the lashings, the skin -- and what do living trees do under
that load over years?

:::box do "An experiment you can run"
With an arborist and an engineer: instrument the anchor trees of a sky web with
simple tilt gauges, log their movement through wind and seasons, and weigh every
strut as it goes up. Record every lift's time. Publish the log, including the
lifts that went wrong.
:::

:::box check "For checkers"
Arborists especially: what do the sky-web chapters not yet consider about the
anchor trees' health, roots and long-term response?
:::
