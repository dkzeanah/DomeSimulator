**The open question:** can the seams of a dome manage its own moisture -- sorting
air by dew point, draining at the rim, keeping wood dry -- with a small controller,
a fan, desiccant drawers and cold plates?

:::box do "An experiment you can run"
Fit two cheap temperature-and-humidity loggers: one in a seam channel, one in the
room. Log through a season. Compute the dew point of each from its readings, and
mark every day the seam's surface fell below the room's dew point. That is the
condition the whole seam-climate system exists to prevent.
:::

:::box check "For checkers"
The seam-climate chapters are modelled, not measured. Are their caveats -- what has
not been built -- as prominent as their arithmetic?
:::
