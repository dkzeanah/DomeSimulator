**The open question:** who is this for, really -- and what would a small home you
owned, could improve as you went, and could take with you have meant to somebody
starting out? The author's own answer, and Buckminster Fuller's, are in these
chapters.

:::box do "An experiment you can run"
Ask three people who might need a building like this -- a young family, a single
parent, somebody starting a business -- what they would use one for first, and
what would stop them. Write down their words, not yours. Compare them with the
catalogue.
:::

:::box check "For checkers"
Does the book make its case for who it is for without over-promising what a dome
can do for a family?
:::
