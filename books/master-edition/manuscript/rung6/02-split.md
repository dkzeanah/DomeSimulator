**The open question:** is the measured splitting rate typical -- and what is an
hour at the log really worth against an hour's wage?

:::box do "An experiment you can run"
Time ten full splitting sessions, with the same saw and the same chain filing
routine, and log the wedges from each, the fuel used and how you felt at the end.
Plot your rate against session number. Then compute your own rate's value against
bought lumber, exactly as the hour chapter does.
:::

:::box check "For checkers"
Is the measured rate described with enough detail -- saw, chain, log size, session
length -- for somebody else to repeat it?
:::
