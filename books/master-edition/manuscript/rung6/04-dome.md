**The open question:** can you derive every number in the dome yourself, and does
it agree with the software that printed it?

:::box do "An experiment you can run"
From the twelve corners of an icosahedron, derive the two chord factors by hand --
the volumes show four independent routes. Then take the paper dome from rung two,
measure its edges, and compute the radius they imply. How close is the paper to
the arithmetic? For the brave: run the project's own software and compare.
:::
