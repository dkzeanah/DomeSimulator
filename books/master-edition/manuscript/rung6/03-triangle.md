**The open question:** how rough can the frame be before it stops forgiving? The
Frankendome -- built from mismatched salvage with no two members alike -- stood
for months, and it is the project's evidence and its warning at once.

:::box do "An experiment you can run"
Under an engineer's guidance, build two test panels: one from your best-graded
wedges and one from wedges you would normally reject. Load each the same way, the
same small amount, and measure how much it moves. Never load anything to failure
near a person, and never test a panel anyone will build into a home.
:::

Do the stories of the earlier domes draw the right lesson -- that triangles forgive,
but that forgiveness is not a structural rating?
:::
