**The open question:** what does a real build cost, start to finish, in hours and in
money -- measured, not estimated -- and where were the books wrong?

:::box do "An experiment you can run"
Publish your build's log: every stage's hours, every receipt, every rejected wedge,
every rain day. Then write the chapter the paperback ends with -- what you would do
differently -- and send it to the author. The next edition is built from logs like
yours.
:::

:::box check "For checkers"
Find one number in either volume you think is wrong and say why. Corrections are
printed in this project, not hidden.
:::
