**The open question:** what nearly went wrong? Safety improves fastest when the near
misses are written down, not just the accidents.

:::box do "An experiment you can run"
Keep a near-miss log for your whole build: date, what happened, what nearly
happened, why, and what you changed. Share it with the author and with the next
family. Every rule in rung four's safety card was once somebody's near miss.
:::
