# What to Check

If you have been asked to check this book, this page is where to spend your
time. The questions below are gathered from the end of every card on rungs four
to six -- each one a place where the author most wants a second pair of eyes --
with the page it comes from.

Four kinds of check are worth more than any others:

* **Wrong numbers.** A figure that matches neither column of the two-domes page,
  a sum that does not add up, a count that disagrees with another count.
* **Unsafe instructions.** Anything about felling, the saw, ladders, lifting,
  hanging loads, oil rags or electricity that you would not want followed exactly
  as written.
* **Claims stated as more certain than they are.** Every figure in this project
  is meant to say whether it was measured, estimated or declared. Find one that
  does not.
* **Lost readers.** The page where you stopped understanding, or stopped caring.
  Write down which, and why.
