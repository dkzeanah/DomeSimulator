# Two Books, Two Domes

The two volumes in this book are about the same method and the same frame -- a
two-frequency hemisphere of {{dome.panels}} panels and {{dome.members}} split-log
members, joined by the pinwheel, with {{dome.seams}} seams and two key profiles.
They are not about the same *dome*. This page sets them side by side so a reader
checking the work can see every difference, and why each one exists. Every
figure in the table is computed by the software each book is built from.

## How to read it

**Neither dome is wrong.** Volume A builds the owner's real dome, sized from the
members as they actually measure. Volume B works a dome out from a tree, sized so
that the longest stick one person cuts and carries is exactly six feet. Because a
pinwheel member stops short of its corner, "a six-foot edge" and "a six-foot
stick" are two different domes, and the paperback's is the bigger one.

**The forty hours are not the same forty hours.** Volume A's clock starts at the
log and ends with the frame standing, and comes to {{hr.total}} hours with the
allowance. Volume B's {{tt_hr.with_error}} hours are the erection alone; its
splitting comes on top. A reader comparing the two titles should compare
{{hr.total}} with {{tt_hr.total}}, not {{hr.total}} with {{tt_hr.with_error}}.
Both books also report the hours for the whole build, which is longer than
either clock.

**What a checker can do with this page.** If you find a figure in either volume
that matches neither column, it is a mistake, and it is the most useful thing you
can mark in this copy.
