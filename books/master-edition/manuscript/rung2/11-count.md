On rung one the builder counted the hours. This chapter counts the way a builder
counts -- by measuring a little and working out a lot -- and then looks at the
most important question to ask about any number: *was it measured, or guessed?*

Here is the real forty-hour clock from Volume A. Read the last column: it says
how each number was found.

    {{clock.table}}

Splitting is **measured**: the owner timed real sessions at the log. Cutting,
building panels and raising are **estimates**: careful, reasoned, but not yet
timed on a real build. The last line is the owner's own **allowance** for the
mistakes every first build makes. A book that hid the difference would look
more certain and be less true.

:::box do "Make it"
**You need:** a timer, paper, scissors, tape, a notebook, and your paper-dome
jig from chapter six.

1. **Measure.** Time yourself making {{mb.timed}} triangles on the jig. Write
   down the time for *each* triangle separately.
2. **Work it out.** The dome needs forty. Forty is {{mb.timed_times}} lots
   of {{mb.timed}}. Multiply your total. Write it down and label it *estimate*.
3. **Learning curve.** Look at your five separate times. Was the first one the
   slowest? Draw a little bar chart of the five.
4. **Measure again.** Now actually make the next five, and time them. Was your
   estimate high or low?
5. **Label everything.** Go back through your notebook and write *measured* or
   *estimated* next to every number.
:::

:::box why "Why it works"
Most of a build's time is repetition, and repetition gets faster: the first
panel off a jig is a rehearsal and the fourth is usually the first good one. An
estimate made from the first few is therefore usually high -- unless it forgets
the learning time, the tired afternoon or the rain day, which is what the
allowance is for. The forty hours cover the frame; the whole build, with the
skin, floor and openings, is about {{money.labour_hours}} hours, and the books
keep both numbers in view.
:::

:::box done "You've got it when"
Every number in your notebook is labelled measured or estimated, and you can
say which kind the real clock's splitting time is.
:::
