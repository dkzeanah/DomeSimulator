# How This Book Works

This book can be read three ways, and none of them is front to back.

## Up a ladder of six rungs

A **rung** is a depth. Every rung tells the whole idea, start to finish, at its
own level, so you can stop on any rung and still have all of it.

| Rung | | For | It gets you to |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Read It Together** | a grown-up and a child | the whole story, in twelve bedtimes |
| 2 | **Make It Small** | children of eight and up, any beginner | the dome built at kitchen-table size |
| 3 | **Plan It** | teenagers and adults | a one-page plan for your own building |
| 4 | **Build It** | adults with tools and help | a real frame, closed against the weather |
| 5 | **Make It Yours** | anyone who wants a different one | a costed design of your own |
| 6 | **Push It Further** | builders, students, engineers | an experiment, run and published |

Rungs one to three are written in full here. Rungs four to six are **routes**:
short cards that send you through the two volumes at the back, in order, with
page numbers.

## Along twelve threads

A **thread** is one idea followed up all six rungs: the tree, the split, the
triangle, the dome, the joint, the panel, the raising, the skin, the ground, the
uses, the count, and taking care. Every chapter ends with a line that shows where
its thread goes one rung **down** (simpler), one rung **up** (deeper) and to the
**thread page** at the back, which lists every page in this book -- the rungs and
both volumes -- where that idea appears. Climb one thread alone and you will
understand that one part completely.

## Across three reads

The first book of this project was written so that a reader could follow just one
kind of chapter -- the story, the manual or the explanation -- and still get a
dome. This edition keeps all three inside every chapter on the first three rungs:

* **the story** is the plain text;
* **the doing** is in the green boxes -- *Try it together*, *Make it*,
  *Worksheet*;
* **the why** is in the blue boxes -- *For the grown-up*, *Why it works*, *Why*.

Read only the green boxes and you have a project book. Read only the blue ones and
you have the explanation. Read only the text and you have the story.

## Milestones

Each rung has milestones -- things you can do, not things you have read. They are
listed with their rungs, and gathered on one page at the back as a ladder a family
can tick off. There are {{mb.milestones}} in all, from a walk to find three
triangles to an experiment published for the next family.

## One rule above all

Anything with a saw, a ladder, a heavy lift or a hanging load is grown-up work,
done with the right gear and never alone. Anything that will carry people, snow
or wind is checked by an engineer before it is built. The safety thread -- the
last chapter on every rung -- is the one to read first.
