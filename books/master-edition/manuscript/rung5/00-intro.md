The reference build is one answer. This rung is every other one the project has
worked out: other trees and sections, hubs instead of pinwheels, other shapes and
sizes, other skins, other grounds, and fifteen different buildings on the same
frame -- each priced from the same models, so a choice is a comparison and not a
preference.

Each card says what you can change on that thread, sets a small design exercise,
and routes you into the catalogues and price lists in the volumes. The milestone
for this rung is a design of your own with a price built line by line.

A design is not a building. Anything you change that carries a load -- a bigger
dome, a heavier skin, a different frame material, a dome hung from trees -- goes
back to the engineer on your safety plan before it is built.
