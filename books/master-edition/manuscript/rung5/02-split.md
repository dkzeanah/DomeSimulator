The solid split wedge is the reference member, not the only one. Rectangular
sticks, bought lumber and the whole catalogue of frame materials and sections are
priced on the same dome, so you can see exactly what the wedge buys and what it
gives up.

:::box do "Design exercise"
Price the reference frame twice: once in split wedges from your own trees, once
in bought rectangular lumber. What does the difference pay for, and what does it
cost you in hours?
:::

:::
