Every dome builder chooses between hubs and no hubs. A hub makes every corner the
same factory part and needs identical struts; the pinwheel needs no connector and
costs extra wood. Both are good systems for different builders.

:::box do "Design exercise"
Decide which system suits you, from three questions: who cuts the wood, who
assembles it, and whether the dome will ever be taken apart. Write your three
answers and your choice.
:::

:::box check "For checkers"
Is the extra wood the hubless frame uses -- every panel owning all three of its
members -- stated plainly wherever the method's savings are claimed?
:::
