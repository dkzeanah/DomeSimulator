Designing safe is mostly knowing what to hand to whom: what an engineer needs to
see, what the building department needs to read, and how to build so that a
part can be changed later without weakening the rest.

:::box do "Design exercise"
Write the one page you would send an engineer about your design: its size, its
frame, its skin, its ground, its loads, and the questions you need answered.
:::

:::box check "For checkers"
If you are an engineer: what would you need to see that the books do not yet
provide?
:::
