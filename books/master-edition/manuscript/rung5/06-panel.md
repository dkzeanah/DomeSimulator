Panels decide what the building does. A doorway is a whole panel left out and
framed; a window replaces part of a panel and re-triangulates around the glass; a
panel can be a skylight, a louvre, a flue or a shelf.

:::box do "Design exercise"
Take your plan from rung three. Mark which of the forty triangles are windows,
which is the door, which is the flue and which stay blank. Count the special
panels.
:::
