The method was never pine's; it was the sector's. Other species split, dry and
last differently, an oval trunk splits by area rather than by angle, and a tree's
value depends entirely on what you ask it to be.

List the species that grow within a short drive of you. For each, find whether it
splits straight, whether it resists rot in the ground, and how fast it dries. Pick
the one you would build from, and say why.
:::
