The owner's way of building is in the air: a web of wire rope between living
trees, a battery winch, each triangle lifted flat and joined in place, and the
finished dome left hanging where the trees hold it.

:::box do "Design exercise"
Walk your land and find four trees that could form a square of about the size
the volumes use. Measure their trunks and note their species and health. Would you
trust them -- and who would you ask?
:::

:::box check "For checkers"
The sky web is the owner's plan, partly tested. Are its measured parts -- the
weighed struts, the winch -- kept clearly apart from its modelled parts and its
open questions?
:::
