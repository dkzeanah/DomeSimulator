Style it: the same frame dressed every way the Dome Creator can dress it --
twelve styles rendered, every panel material on every frame material, every
cladding on every ground -- each one a priced rebuild of the same dome.

:::box do "Design exercise"
Pick the style closest to what you want. Then change one menu at a time -- the
panel, the cladding, the ground -- and write down how the price moves. Which menu
matters most for your design?
:::

:::box check "For checkers"
The price tables are rebuilt by software. Pick any three prices and check whether
the materials they assume are realistic where you live.
:::
