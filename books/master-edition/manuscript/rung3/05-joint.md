:::box why "Why"
Only two edge lengths exist because the dome is an icosahedron subdivided once:
every edge is either half an original edge or a line between two midpoints. The
cut lengths differ from the chords by the pinwheel's bite, which is set by how
wide the stick is -- a fatter log means a bigger bite and shorter members for the
same dome. The fold angle plus the gap the key fills always adds up to the
{{cut.sector}}-degree wedge the tree was split into, which is why two keys close
the whole dome.
:::

:::box watch "Watch out"
Never cut a member to its chord: it will be too long by the bite, forty times
over. Keep the two lengths in separate, marked stacks. The short-sided panels
have a left and a right side that differ by about two inches over five feet --
pick them from their stacks in order, never by eye. And the volumes print more
than one cut list; the comparison page at the back says which is which.
:::
