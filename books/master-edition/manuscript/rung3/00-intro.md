Rungs one and two were about understanding. This rung is about deciding. Every
chapter is one decision a family has to make before any real tree is cut, and
each one ends with a **worksheet**. Fill in all twelve and you have a plan on
one page: what you are building, how big, from which trees, by when, for how
much, and how safely.

Each chapter gives the **reference answer** first -- what the project's own
build chose, with its real numbers -- and then the space for yours. The
reference is not the right answer for everybody. It is a worked example, so you
can see every sum done once before you do your own.

Two things are worth knowing before you start.

**The two books chose different domes.** The Complete Edition (Volume A) builds
the owner's dome, {{dome.diameter_ft}} feet across. The paperback (Volume B)
works a dome out from a measured tree, and gets {{tt_dome.diameter_ft}} feet.
Both are right; they start from different places. Chapter four is about exactly
that, and the comparison page at the back puts the two side by side.

**Every number here says what it is.** *Measured* means somebody timed it or
weighed it. *Estimated* means it was reasoned out and not yet measured.
*Declared* means it is a choice or a price somebody wrote down, with a reason.
Your own plan should use the same three words.
