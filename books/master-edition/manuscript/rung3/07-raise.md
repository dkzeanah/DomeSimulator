:::box why "Why"
A dome is a mechanism until it closes and a structure afterwards: before the top
panels go in, each course needs to be held; after, every triangle holds every
other. Lifting finished panels instead of loose sticks is what makes one person's
raising possible at all.
:::

:::box watch "Watch out"
Never stand under anything that is hanging -- a triangle, a dome, or the rope that
holds them. Rigging, anchor trees and winches carry real loads that no book can
rate; that is an engineer's job and an arborist's. Raise in calm weather only:
a half-closed dome is a sail.
:::
