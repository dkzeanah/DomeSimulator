Your books

Every book in the project is on this shelf. The ones already written keep their own builder (shown underneath each), and their newest printed copy opens in the Reader. New books are made here: pick a kind, name the threads (the ideas, characters or subjects that run through the whole book), and the composer lays out the levels each thread climbs -- from a story, to the theory, to a working template, to steps, to something a family does together.

The graph

Each dot is a chapter; lines are how chapters relate. Drag dots to arrange them. To link two, press Link, click the first, then the second.

The page

Pictures and text boxes are placed on the chapter's page. Drag to move, drag the corner to size. Pick one to set its caption.
