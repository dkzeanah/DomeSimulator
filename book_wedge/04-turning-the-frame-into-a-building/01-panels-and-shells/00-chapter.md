# 11. Panels and Shells

The frame holds the building up. The skin keeps the weather out. They are separate jobs and this method keeps them separate, which is why the skin can be changed without touching the structure.

One number governs the whole chapter: no panel of this dome fits on a four-foot sheet in any rotation. The narrowest is 52.520 inches and a sheet is 48. Everything about how the skin gets built follows from that.
