# 14. Environmental Control

A hemisphere has about 41 percent less envelope than a box of the same floor area, which is the biggest thermal fact about this shape and the reason everything in this chapter is smaller than the equivalent for a conventional building.

The shape gives that back in one place: the upper third of a dome's wall faces the sky, so it behaves like roof in summer. Most of what follows is about spending the saving well and not losing it through the top.
