# 13. Utilities

A conventional house runs its services in wall cavities. A dome does not have a wall cavity, and the attempts to pretend it does are where most of the trouble comes from.

This method puts everything in one column standing in the middle of the building -- $1,405 of it, in through one port in the floor and out through one sleeve at the apex. Three designed penetrations in the whole envelope, all of them gasketed, none of them cut later.
