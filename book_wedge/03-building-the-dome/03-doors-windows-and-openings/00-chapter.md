# 10. Doors, Windows, and Openings

Every triangle in a dome is carrying something, so every opening is a decision about where its load goes instead.

The structure is usually the easiest part. An opening is also a hole in the envelope, a hole in the insulation, a path for water and an edge that has to be finished, and those four take longer to solve than the framing does.
