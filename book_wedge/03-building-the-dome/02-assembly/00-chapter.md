# 9. Assembly

Assembly is the short part. If the panels are made and checked, raising a nineteen-foot dome is a day or two with two people.

Everything here assumes you built all forty panels first. The method is arranged so that nearly all the work happens flat, at waist height, where it can be checked -- and then the building goes up quickly because there is nothing left to figure out.
