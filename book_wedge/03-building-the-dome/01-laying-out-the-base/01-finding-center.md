# Finding center

Everything on the site is measured from one stake.

Put it in properly, because you cannot move it later without moving the
building.

WHAT THE CENTRE IS

It is the point directly below the apex, and it is the point every base vertex
is the same distance from.

On the reference build every base vertex is 116.4984 inches from it -- the
sphere radius -- measured horizontally along the ground.

HOW TO SET IT

Drive a stake. Put a nail in the top of it, dead centre, and use the nail as
the point rather than the stake. A stake is an inch across; a nail is a point.

Then leave it alone. Everything references it and anything that knocks it
moves the whole building.

WHAT TO CHECK FIRST

Before you commit: stand at the centre and turn round.

Is there room? The dome is 19.416 feet across, which means 9.708 feet in every
direction from where you are standing, plus room to work outside that, plus
room for the eaves of whatever you skin it with.

Check the low side. Ground that looks flat is rarely flat, and a dome needs
its base ring level all the way round.

Check what is above. A dome is 9.708 feet tall at the apex and branches do not
move.

THE THING PEOPLE SKIP

Check the centre against the services.

The reference build brings power, water and drain up through a single port in
the middle of the floor. That port is at the centre stake. If there is a rock,
a root or an existing pipe there, you want to know before the frame is up and
not after.
