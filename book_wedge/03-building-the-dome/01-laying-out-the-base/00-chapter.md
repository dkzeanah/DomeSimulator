# 8. Laying Out the Base

A dome is laid out from one point and one distance. Get those two right and the rest of the site work is bookkeeping.

The rule that runs through this chapter: measure everything from a datum, never from the last thing you measured. Chained measurements accumulate error. A datum does not.
