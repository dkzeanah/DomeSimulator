# 77. Every Skin on Every Frame

The last chapter changed one menu at a time. This one changes two at once,
in full: every panel on every frame material, and every cladding on every
foundation. Each cell is the whole cabin rebuilt and priced -- not a sum of
the one-at-a-time rows, though for menus that do not touch each other it
comes out the same.

## Every combination, priced

Read across for the frame material, down for the panel; the number is the
cabin's total, in dollars. Each frame material is made in its own section,
as in chapter {{ch.stymenus}}.

## Cladding on foundation

Read across for the foundation, down for the cladding layer over the
cabin's plywood panels:

    {{sty.grid2}}

The bare cabin's own combination is one cell of this table. The treehouse
platform column sits above every other, whatever is on the roof; the green
roof and the poured concrete shell rows sit above every other, whatever the
dome stands on.

## How many domes that is

These two tables are a few hundred buildings. The Creator's menus -- four
frequencies, six frame styles, eight strut shapes, eight frame materials,
sixteen panels, three stacked cladding layers, seven foundations and the
rest -- multiply to {{sty.shells}} shells. Some settings do nothing in some
combinations, a hub style on a frame with no hubs, for one, and taking those
out leaves {{sty.distinct}} genuinely different shells. Furnish the floor and
the room menus multiply to {{sty.fitouts}} fit-outs on top, and the four
dials -- radius, strut width, recess and foundation size -- add
{{sty.dials}} settings each.

Nobody will price them all, and nobody needs to: the tables in this Part are
built so you can find your own combination between two you can see. And the
Creator itself, in the launcher, prices any one of them the moment you pick
it.
