# 78. The Price List, Item by Item

Every dollar in the last four chapters is one of the numbers on this page
multiplied by a quantity the Dome Creator measured. Here are the numbers.

## Declared, not measured

None of these prices was measured. They are what the Creator's catalogue
says, typed in by a person, each a reasonable figure for its item at the
time it was written. Your supplier's price is the right one. Change a price
in the catalogue and every table in this Part changes with it the next time
the book is built -- that is the whole reason they are printed: so that you
can see which numbers you would change.

## Wire, pipe and walls

The fit-out runs on four rates: wire at ${{sty.wire}} a metre, run in
conduit; PEX at ${{sty.pex}} a metre for each of hot and cold; drain at
${{sty.drain}} a metre; and ${{sty.rough}} to rough in each fixture. A floor
section can be assigned to any of {{sty.room_count}} uses -- {{sty.rooms}} --
and partitioned with markings, low walls or full walls.

The fit-out is where a small building's money quietly goes. A cabin whose
whole shell cost a few thousand dollars can carry a fit-out that costs as
much again, and draw more power than a small solar array makes. Chapter

the wedge method actually builds; this list is everything else you might
put on the floor.
