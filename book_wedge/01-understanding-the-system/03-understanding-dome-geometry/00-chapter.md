# 3. Understanding Dome Geometry

The wedge method is a way of building a dome, but a dome is a shape first and a method second. This chapter is the shape.

None of it is specific to wedges. Everything here is true of any 2V dome built by anybody out of anything, and if you have built one before you can skim it. What you cannot skip is the last section but one, on dihedral angles, because the whole method rests on a number that appears there and does not appear anywhere else.
