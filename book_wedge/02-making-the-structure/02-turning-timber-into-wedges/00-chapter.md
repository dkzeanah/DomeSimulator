# 5. Turning Timber Into Wedges

This is the chapter where a tree becomes a building material, and it is the one that has no equivalent in a normal dome book.

A strut-and-hub builder buys lumber. Here you are making the lumber, and the shape you make it into is what carries the dome's geometry. Get this chapter right and the assembly is straightforward. Get it wrong and no amount of care later will recover it.
