# 6. Making the Struts

Cutting a hundred and twenty sticks is not a hundred and twenty problems. It is about six problems, repeated, and this chapter is the six.

The one worth reading twice is the first. The chord is not the cut, and every dome that has ever been built a hundred and twenty sticks too short was built by somebody who assumed it was.
