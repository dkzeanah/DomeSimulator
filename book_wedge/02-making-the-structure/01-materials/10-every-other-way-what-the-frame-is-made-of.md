# Every other way: what the frame is made of

The same 120 members, in each material this project can price. The volume is the reference build's own: a 45-degree sector of a 12-inch log, 664 feet of it.

EVERY OTHER WAY OF DOING IT

                            cost     weight
    Galvanized Steel  $   40,593   31,961 lb   7,850 kg/m3 at $2.80/kg
    Structural Steel  $   46,391   31,961 lb   7,850 kg/m3 at $3.20/kg
    Rebar Steel       $   27,545   31,961 lb   7,850 kg/m3 at $1.90/kg
    Aluminum          $   37,398   10,993 lb   2,700 kg/m3 at $7.50/kg
    Timber (SPF)      $    1,773    1,954 lb   480 kg/m3 at $2.00/kg
    Whole Tree Trunk  $    1,080    2,646 lb   650 kg/m3 at $0.90/kg   <- the reference build
    PVC Pipe          $    8,791    5,700 lb   1,400 kg/m3 at $3.40/kg
    Bamboo            $    2,128    2,931 lb   720 kg/m3 at $1.60/kg

The dearest of these is 42.9 times the cheapest. That is the size of the decision, and it is why the book states which one it assumes rather than leaving it to a reader to infer.

These change the frame itself. Every other choice in this book can be made after the dome is standing; these cannot be made twice.

WHERE THESE NUMBERS COME FROM

materials.FRAME_MATERIALS, applied to the reference build's own quantities. Nothing on this page is a rate quoted from outside the project, and nothing on it was typed: regenerate the whole table with `py -3.12 -m wedge_book.alternatives`.

There is no straw-bale row and no earthbag row, because this project cannot cost one. A table with a plausible number in it that came from nowhere is worse than a table with a gap.
