# 4. Materials

The method works with bought lumber, with sawn timber from a local mill, and with logs off your own land. It was designed for the third.

The reason is one number. Split a trunk into wedges and you keep about 88 percent of it; mill the same trunk into 2x4s and you keep about 45. Squaring a round log means throwing the round part away, and this method does not square anything.
