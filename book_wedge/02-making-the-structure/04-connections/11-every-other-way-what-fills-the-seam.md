# Every other way: what fills the seam

The gap two sawn faces leave is 309 feet long in this dome and it exists whether or not anything is put in it. These are what the solver will put there.

EVERY OTHER WAY OF DOING IT

    
    rigid             a rigid spline; the part is a key 2.80 in across its base   <- the reference build
    hose              a hose, which is also the air and water route; the part is a line up to 2.88 in outside diameter
    none              left open; the part is nothing; the channel stays open
    raw_trapezoid     the raw sector, point truncated; the key is 2.80 in wide   <- the reference build
    shaved_flat       a machined flat key; the key is 1.00 in wide

These change the frame itself. Every other choice in this book can be made after the dome is standing; these cannot be made twice.

WHERE THESE NUMBERS COME FROM

the raw-wedge solver's own configuration space, applied to the reference build's own quantities. Nothing on this page is a rate quoted from outside the project, and nothing on it was typed: regenerate the whole table with `py -3.12 -m wedge_book.alternatives`.

There is no straw-bale row and no earthbag row, because this project cannot cost one. A table with a plausible number in it that came from nowhere is worse than a table with a gap.
