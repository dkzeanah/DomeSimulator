# 7. Connections

The pinwheel joint carries the building through wood pressed against wood. The fasteners are not holding the dome up. They are holding the joint closed so the wood can.

That distinction runs through this whole chapter, and it is why a method with no hubs and no brackets is not missing anything. It is also why the fastener choices here are mostly about getting the joint apart again rather than about strength.
