# 20. Builder's Reference

Everything in this chapter is generated from the solved geometry rather than copied from a table, which means it can be checked and it means it extends.

The two ratios at the top of the strut table are the whole of it: the A chord is 0.618034 times the radius and the B chord 0.546533. Every other number here is those two, a member width, and arithmetic.
