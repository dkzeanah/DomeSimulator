# Surface area

The shell, the cap and the paint are all priced per square foot, so this is
the table the money comes out of.

      diameter    AAA face    BAB face        shell   floor ring   floor circle
    ----------------------------------------------------------------------
         8.00'       2.65       2.23        93.3        47.0          50.3
        12.00'       5.95       5.01       210.0       105.8         113.1
        16.00'      10.59       8.92       373.3       188.1         201.1
        20.00'      16.54      13.93       583.3       293.9         314.2
        19.42'      15.59      13.13       549.8       277.0         296.1
        24.00'      23.82      20.06       839.9       423.2         452.4
        30.00'      37.21      31.34      1312.4       661.3         706.9

WHICH NUMBER TO USE

For the FRAME's shell -- panels, membrane, paint -- use the shell surface.
It is the sum of the forty faces and it is the area of material that has to
exist.

For the CAP, use more than that. A cap stands off the frame, so its area is
larger: the reference build's bare cap is
592.2 square feet against
549.8 of
face area, and every quilted layer under it makes the next one bigger again.

For LAMINATE, add waste. Glass fibre over compound curvature wastes a great
deal, and the reference build's model carries an explicit waste fraction for
exactly this reason.

THE COMPARISON WORTH MAKING

At the reference diameter the shell is about
550 square feet over
277 square feet of floor -- a ratio of
1.98.

A rectangular building of the same floor area with an eight-foot wall and a
flat roof has substantially more, which is where the 41 percent envelope
saving comes from. Less surface is less material, less insulation and less
heat leaving, all at once.

It is the strongest argument this shape has and it is entirely contained in
this table.
