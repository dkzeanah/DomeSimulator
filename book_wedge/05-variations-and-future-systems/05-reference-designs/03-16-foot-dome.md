# 16-foot dome

Sixteen feet is the size most people should build and fewer do, because
twenty sounds better.

THE NUMBERS

    diameter                   16.00 ft
    apex height                 8.00 ft
    A strut, cut length       57.513 in
    B strut, cut length       51.478 in
    floor, inside the ring     188.1 sq ft
    shell surface              373.3 sq ft
    timber in the frame          545 ft

WHAT IT IS FOR

A cabin for one or two people. A serious workshop. A guest building. A
studio with room for the work and the person.

WHY IT IS THE PRACTICAL SWEET SPOT

Members under five feet. Still one-person handling, still short enough to
come out of modest trees, still transportable in anything.

Eight feet to the apex, which is a normal ceiling. You can stand anywhere in
the middle half of the floor.

188 square feet, which is a real room.

And the whole build is within a few weekends rather than a season.

THE HONEST COMPARISON WITH TWENTY

Going from sixteen to twenty adds about
89 square
feet of floor and about
117 feet of timber, and it
moves every member past five feet.

That is worth it if the extra floor is needed. It is not worth it for the
sound of the number, and a sixteen-foot dome finished well is a better
building than a twenty-foot one abandoned at the shell.
