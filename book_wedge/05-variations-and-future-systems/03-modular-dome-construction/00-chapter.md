# 17. Modular Dome Construction

The frame is the same in every building this method makes. What changes is the infill, and that is the whole manufacturing argument.

What makes it hold is a very short list of interfaces -- about six -- that are fixed while everything else is free to vary. The proof it works is that the most expensive component in the design was swapped for an entirely different technology and nothing else in the building had to change.
