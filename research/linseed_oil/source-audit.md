## Verification sources

Consulted September 30, 2026. Manufacturer instructions describe the named formulation; they are not independent test results.

- [danish](https://www.triedandtruewoodfinish.com/products/danish-oil/): Interior application schedule and product-specific food-contact use.
- [wax](https://www.triedandtruewoodfinish.com/products/original-wood-finish/): Separate application schedule for the oil-and-wax blend.
- [faq](https://www.triedandtruewoodfinish.com/resources/faq/): Water resistance and exposure/compatibility limits. Coverage wording differs from the product page; the film uses the product page claim and does not treat it as a measurement.
- [rags](https://www.mass.gov/info-details/disposing-of-oily-rags): Fire-service storage and disposal guidance.
- [fire](https://fireline.seattle.gov/2026/04/02/spring-clean-for-a-safer-household/): Municipal guidance on heat buildup, rag storage and local disposal.
- [wood](https://research.fs.usda.gov/download/treesearch/37432.pdf): FPL discussion of wood species, finish and weather effects on mildew, page 18.
- [bond](https://www.westsystem.com/instruction/epoxy-basics/surface-preparation/): Oil and wax contamination must be removed before bonding.
- [glass](https://images.dap.com/33%20Window%20Glazing%20TDS_5.9.19.pdf): Sash preparation and retainers; plastic panes and IGUs with organic seals are excluded. This sash specification does not establish overhead suitability.

## Editorial decisions

The transcript provides no controlled test establishing superiority to all hardware-store finishes, no reliable financial saving and no evidence of structural restoration. These claims are not repeated. Dome-envelope and joint guidance applies the documented limits to this assembly; it is not a tested new building system.
