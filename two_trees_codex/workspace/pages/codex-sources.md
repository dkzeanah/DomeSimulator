The printed factors and worked results are rounded for reading. Live calculations use generated geometry. The nominal six-foot edge example is not a final cut prescription. The eighty-strut-per-tree example in an earlier timber demonstration uses a different source length and sectioning method from the sixty-four-blank plan here.

The source notes' trunk diameter text includes an apparent feet/inches typo. This draft uses the explicitly named eight-inch and twelve-inch diameter examples as declared mathematical examples, not as measured dimensions of the author's trees. No unverified claim of invention priority, universal recovery percentage, saw capability, or structural rating is established by this manuscript.

Before publication, replace open field prompts, verify the saw identification from the machine, date all prices, reconcile the final counts, update the physical configuration, and have the construction details reviewed for their intended use.
