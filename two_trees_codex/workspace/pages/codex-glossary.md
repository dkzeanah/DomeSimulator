# Glossary — the words beside the cuts

**A / B:** This book's long / short nominal geodesic edge families.

**Blank:** The stock available before a member's final cuts, including necessary process allowance.

**Chord factor:** The ratio of an ideal edge length to sphere radius.

**Decagon:** The ten-sided base polygon of this hemisphere.

**Edge:** A unique line segment in the geometric mesh; not necessarily one wooden member.

**Fold:** The smaller angle between outward normals of adjacent panels.

**Kerf:** Material removed across the width of a saw cut. Bucking kerf affects section-length inventory; ripping kerf affects sector recovery.

**Member:** One finished structural wooden part, with its physical geometry and identity.

**Panel:** One independently assembled triangular frame, owning three members.

**Pinwheel:** A cyclic end-to-side relationship within a panel, with each member receiving one neighbor and terminating against another.

**Radial sector:** A region bounded by two radii and their circular arc; one eighth has a forty-five-degree included angle.

**Seam:** A shared interface between two panels. This hemisphere has fifty-five interior seams and ten exposed base edges.

**Stock envelope:** The longitudinal extent needed to contain the physical member before any extra process allowance.

**Zome:** A related form based on a zonohedral construction; it has its own geometry and is not this spherical 2V frame.
