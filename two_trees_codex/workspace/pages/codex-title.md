# 2 trees: Build your (D)Home

One small chainsaw. One hundred and twenty wedge members. Forty triangles. Two weeks to attempt a frame.

A practical explanation and a builder's field story, beginning with two trees and following the decisions that turn them into a geodesic dome.

This is the working manuscript. The construction described here is a planned build; its outcome will be recorded as it happens. The author describes the intended saw as a Husqvarna 120 Mark III purchased from Lowe's. The exact product label, receipt, and supplied bar-and-chain combination belong in the equipment record before publication.
