# Scene study - Reading the math labels

The source film separates observations, calculations, planning choices and estimates. The labels and values below are reproduced from that lesson to explain its visual language; they are not new entries in this book's build log.

## Read the labels before the totals

- MEASURED   fuel while ripping: 2 tanks an hour, one timed sitting

- MEASURED   cutting rate: 30 struts in a 6-hour afternoon

- DERIVED    ripping time: 120 struts at 5.0 an hour = 24 hours

- DECIDED    the plan: felling days 1-2, bucking days 3-4, ripping days 5-8

- ESTIMATED  the saw's tank: 0.28 to 0.37 L; the listings disagree

- NOT METERED  fuel for felling and bucking: left out, not guessed

## What the displayed conclusion includes

So the fuel is a range, and it is the ripping only. This is the scope of the source lesson's calculation. Compare its assumptions with your own recorded tank size, refill volumes, operating hours and cutting rate before reusing a total.
