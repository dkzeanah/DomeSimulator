# 16. Four ways to place the same wedge

The raw-wedge simulator names each orientation by the direction of the sector point. **Point dome in** directs the point toward the sphere center. **Point dome out** turns it toward the exterior. **Point panel in** turns it toward the center of its own triangular panel. **Point panel out** directs it across the panel boundary.

These labels describe cross-section orientation around the member's long axis. They do not rename the geodesic edge. An A edge remains an A edge while the wood surrounding it changes position. The change can alter which face is available for a seam or a neighboring member's end.

Compare all four with identical nominal dome size, trunk diameter, and seam settings. Use a wide view to see the overall frame and a close end-grain view to understand the interface. Record the configuration on the figure rather than relying on a nickname such as “inside-out.”

The project's original specification uses the curved surface toward the sky and the point inward. Later tools explore the other orientations. The narrative should make this progression visible: the first idea established a starting configuration; inspection then opened a larger design space.

Selecting an orientation is a fabrication decision. Once a representative full-size panel has been checked, mark that orientation on each blank and in the fixture documentation. A correct length installed with an unintended quarter-turn is a different physical assembly.
