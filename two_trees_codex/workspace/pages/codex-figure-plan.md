Every image needs a caption, provenance, and a reason to occupy the page. For artwork, say that it is conceptual. For a simulator plate, name its configuration. For a photograph, record date and subject. For an explanatory diagram, show units and the definitions used.

Choose full-page plates for geometry that needs space and part openers that need a pause. Use smaller images beside a calculation when the reader must compare numbers and shapes at the same time. A photograph of an unmarked pile should not substitute for a measured inventory diagram.
