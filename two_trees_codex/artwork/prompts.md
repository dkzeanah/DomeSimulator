# Artwork record

All artwork used the built-in image generation tool. Original and revised outputs
are preserved with distinct filenames. The book's technical plates come from the
actual DomeSim meshes; generated artwork remains labeled as editorial concepts.

## Cover concept v1 — retained as a concept

Use case: illustration-story. Portrait opening artwork for “2 trees: Build your
(D)Home”: unfinished wooden dome in a woodland clearing, two source trunks, small
orange saw switched off on a stump with its bar covered, natural wood grain, cream
and forest palette, fine ink and watercolor realism, open space above for type.
No text or logos. Conceptual visualization, not documentary photography.

## Workbench v1 — superseded

Use case: illustration-story. Landscape editorial watercolor of eight long radial
pine sectors on an outdoor bench, curved bark outside and two flat radial faces;
one completed triangle nearby, forest and natural light. No people, active saw,
captions, or dimensions. The generated frame used picture-frame-like joints.

## Author's correction

“should be a pinwheel design where the butt end of one struts meets at the side of
the front of the next one. kind of like an 'overlapping' of strut where 2 different
struts remain on each triangle axis”

## Workbench v2 — revision draft

Edit the right triangle into a cyclic pinwheel: exactly three distinct members,
each butt meeting the side near the front of the next, consistent clockwise
end-to-side contact; no mitered corner. Add a separate neighboring triangle so a
shared boundary retains two parallel members, one from each triangle. Preserve
the forest, bench, raw wood, and watercolor style. No labels or dimensions.

## Workbench v3 — model-guided revision draft

Edit v2 with the simulator's colored pinwheel-pair plate as authoritative geometry.
Arrange two independent triangles edge-to-edge as a diamond: four exterior
members, two distinct central members, six total. Preserve every cyclic end-to-side
contact. Use natural wood instead of diagram colors. Preserve the left raw wedges,
bench, forest, lake, and lighting. No generic miter corners or merged center member.

## Pinwheel plate material study

Edit the actual simulator plate while preserving all geometry, member endpoints,
contacts, silhouettes and the doubled central pair. Only refine timber material
with grain under the existing ochre, green and brown tints. Keep original text,
add “Panel 1 member” and “Panel 2 member” ownership labels with leaders. Do not
redesign the frame or replace contacts with miters. This remains a generative
illustration; the source render remains available for precise comparison.

## Editorial use

The original cover and workbench generations do not establish joint geometry.
The revised workbench has the doubled center but still needs corner review.
They are kept in the image library as drafts; the reading manuscript uses the
unaltered simulator geometry for technical explanations. The pinwheel plate material
study preserved the visible source member arrangement and labels both central
members separately. It is included as an illustrated plate beside Chapter 17,
with the original source render retained for comparison.
