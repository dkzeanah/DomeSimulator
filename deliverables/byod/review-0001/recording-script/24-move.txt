Semi-nomadic means a house that comes apart, not a caravan you tow away on a whim. My working estimate is two days at the quick end, up to two weeks for something large or heavily layered. That is a burst of work: disassemble, transport, reassemble, connect. You spend that effort when a move is worth it. The rest of the time, you are living in a house. A different park, city, or state becomes possible without leaving the house behind. Site agreements still matter, but the improvements to your home travel with you.
