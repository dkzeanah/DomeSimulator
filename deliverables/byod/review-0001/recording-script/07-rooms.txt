Split a big bedroom to make an office. Three years later you retire, stop needing the office, and open the bedroom back up. I live in a trailer. Four bedrooms, living room, den, office, two bathrooms, kitchen, dining area. We have all the allotments of a domicile. But the way we need that space has changed. I was gone for six years. I came back. Three people becomes four, and eventually that same house could be too much for one person. A dome with movable, nonstructural partitions gives us a way to reallocate the space. The shell stays. The room plan can change.
