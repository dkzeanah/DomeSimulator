So what is a pad? Start with the ground, then a deck or prepared surface, and an acceptor rim that the dome latches to. The basic rim can be fixed. This upgrade is an iris: leaves moving on a track, opening and closing like a camera aperture. Watch the contact points move inward, then outward. The concept spans roughly twenty to forty feet. The deck stays in place while the acceptor changes size. A host could accommodate several dome sizes with one adjustable assembly. That mechanism is a development concept, and it belongs in the upgrade column.
