Then there is the top-line version: a rotating foundation, with the tracking built into it. This is the expensive one. And honestly, it is just freaking cool. Imagine some days I want the window looking out on the street or the highway. Some days I have someone over, and I want it facing the back corner of the lot instead, where the fence is high. Imagine I have no solar panels at all. Maybe I like the sun in my face in the morning. Maybe I get tired of that and want the opposite, so I face away from it entirely. I imagine there is a lot to be gained from a rotational house in that manner, and more again with panels covering certain parts of the shell asymmetrically. But the bearing, the drive and the rotating utility connections all need their own design. This is future deluxe hardware, at deluxe pricing.
