When I was nineteen I was able to borrow five grand against my name, for college. I blew that, joined the military, and now at thirty-two I am back for revenge. Constructive revenge, apparently involving domes. I did not fail out because I was stupid. I had no overarching support, I was not stable in my living situations, and I could not focus on the thing that mattered in that time: school. It cost me greatly. Tremendously, if you ask me. What a dome would have done for my eighteen-year-old self would have been revolutionary. That is how I am designing this, and going forward.
