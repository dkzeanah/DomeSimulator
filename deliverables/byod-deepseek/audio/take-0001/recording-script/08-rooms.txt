Four bedrooms, a living room, a den, an office, two bathrooms, a kitchen and a dining area. We have all the allotments of a domicile. But much of that space could be reallocated more efficiently, because the way we need it changed over time. I was gone for six years. I came back. Three people became four, and eventually the same house will be too much for one person. The dome does not have that problem. The shell stays. The room plan can change.
