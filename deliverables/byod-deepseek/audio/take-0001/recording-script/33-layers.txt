Some of my own practices make their way into these domes. I stood lookout outside the skin of a ship in the Arctic Circle, for hours at a time, over months. There is exactly one way to stay warm out there: layers on layers on layers, and then a wind-breaking, watertight suit over all of it. You end up looking like the Michelin man. You also stay alive. It was not that bad. A dome with a removable shell can work the same way. Lift the shell, add a quilted layer, put the shell back over it. Quilted recycled fabric is one of the largest waste streams there is.
