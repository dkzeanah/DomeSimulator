## 02. Why build this way

Time: 00:00:32.300 - 00:01:15.000

Paid for once, used for decades.

Why build like this? Because a channel, once it is in the wall, can be used again and again for different jobs. The network that glues the house together while you build it carries rain to a tank while you live in it, floods the sheathing when a wildfire comes, and finds the leak when something fails. This design lists 31 uses for 14 networks: 3 while you build, 14 while you live in it, 8 to protect it, and 6 to look after it. Every use after the first costs a valve and a few lines of code, not a new wall.

On-screen math:

- uses: 31 across 14 networks
- build 3, live 14, protect 8, maintain 6
- 2.2 uses per network (counted from the design's list)

## 04. A groove in every round face

## 29. How a wildfire takes a house

## 34. A cage is its biggest hole

## 35. Fog, not pepper

## 43. The price of an address

Valves and joints.
