## 03. A groove in every round face

## 10. The insulation splits the stack

## 24. A cage is its biggest hole

Time: 00:13:41.412 - 00:14:25.124

Bond every seam, or it is not a cage.

Bond a mesh into one layer and the dome is a Faraday cage: lightning goes round the people inside, and radio is cut down. But a cage is only as good as its biggest hole. Quarter-inch mesh stops everything below 23.6 gigahertz, and cuts Wi-Fi by about 20 decibels. An unbonded seam is a slot 1.8 metres long that lets in everything above 82 megahertz. An open door lets in everything above 167. So bond all 55 seams, 309 feet of it, and give the door a conductive gasket.
