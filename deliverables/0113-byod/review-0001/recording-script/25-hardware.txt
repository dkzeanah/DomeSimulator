Take this design and change only its radius. The small version is about three hundred square feet. The largest here is about two thousand. The model keeps the same counts of struts, hubs, and panels. Its hardware bill stays the same, while the stick lengths and skin area grow. That illustrates the opportunity. It does not prove a connector rated for the small house can hold up the large one. The hardware has to be qualified for the whole intended size and load range first.
