Amber is the pad host: the deck, the service connections, the meter. Cyan is the dome owner: the house and everything they do to it. The important line stays in the same place. The host provides the site. The resident owns the home. You still pay for the ground and the services, but the house you improve is yours. Both sides keep control of their own part, with fewer things held in common and a clearer goodbye when it is time to move.
