# 3V 5/8 panel dome, 4.88 m -- showcase (sketch-claude)

Rendered by Dome Sketch (sketch-claude) from the same model the plan set is drawn from.

## The figures

- 4,877 mm (16' 0") across the base, 3,120 mm (10' 2-13/16") from the deck to the top
- 18.1 m² of floor (195 ft²)
- 105 panels: 99 whole and 4 partial round the door
- 309 sticks in 28 distinct cuts, from 77 8 ft 2x3s
- 32 sheets of plywood skin, 5 windows and a 5-panel skylight
