# Campaign Digital Media Asset Prompts

Every image asset the launch needs, one self-contained prompt per asset —
each one pastes whole into ChatGPT or Gemini and stands alone (the style
line and the geometry guardrails are repeated inside every prompt so the
paste pump can fire them back to back).

Shared rules, repeated in spirit everywhere: no text, no letters, no
logos, no watermark; five triangles at the top; triangles, never
hexagons; no metal star hubs; bark on the outside face; split-log
members, not milled boards. Caption everything as an illustration.

**Crops:** masthead 21:9 · thumbnail 16:9 · tier cards 1:1 · stories 9:16
· posts 1:1 · email header 6:1.
