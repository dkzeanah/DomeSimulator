# Gemini image prompt — "Frankendome" hero graphic

Paste everything in the fenced block into Gemini. Ask for 16:9 first; then re-run
with "same image, 9:16 vertical, dome centred in the upper two-thirds" for the phone cut.

```
Create a bold, high-energy social media hero image. Photoreal with a cinematic,
slightly stylised finish — like a festival poster crossed with a real build photo.

FRONT AND CENTER: a small 2-frequency (2V) geodesic dome hemisphere, about 6 feet
tall, sitting on a simple timber ring on the ground. It is a "stem cell" dome — the
small seed building a bigger dome grows from. Every strut is a raw chainsaw-cut
timber WEDGE with a TRIANGULAR cross-section: the sharp point of each triangle faces
INWARD toward the centre of the dome, and the wide FLAT face of each wedge faces
OUTWARD, so the outside of the frame reads as a clean faceted skin of flat timber
faces meeting in tight seams. Show that clearly: at one cutaway strut in the
foreground, the triangular end-grain is visible, point aimed at the dome's middle.
No metal hubs — the wedges meet directly at the joints. The wood is rough, honest,
hand-made: visible saw marks, a few amber blobs of glue squeezed out at joints,
one or two mismatched patched panels. It is lovingly imperfect.

THIS IS THE FRANKENDOME, AND IT IS HAVING A PARTY. The triangular panels between
the struts are a patchwork of every possible skin at once: some glowing
translucent LED panels cycling magenta, cyan, electric violet, lime and hot orange;
some clear greenhouse-glass panels; one painted forest green; one made of
cardboard; a couple left open. LED strip lights run along the timber edges in
chasing rainbow colours. Warm light spills from inside the dome onto the ground.
Confetti, soft haze, lens flare, a few people silhouetted dancing around it.

A small glowing bioluminescent jellyfish mascot named Lumen floats beside the dome,
translucent cyan bell and trailing tentacles, looking delighted.

TEXT (large, clean, legible, spelled exactly): "FRANKENDOME!" in chunky warm-amber
letters arched above the dome. Smaller underneath: "2V WEDGE DOME — built from raw
timber". No other text, no logos, no numbers.

COLOUR & MOOD: night scene, deep indigo sky, the dome is the brightest thing in
frame. Dominant accents warm amber (#FFB13E) and cyan, with a full LED rainbow
bonanza on the panels. Joyful, handmade, maker-culture, "you could build this".

COMPOSITION: dome dead centre, low three-quarter hero angle looking slightly up,
shallow depth of field on the foreground wedge cutaway, generous margins so it
crops cleanly to square and vertical.

AVOID: metal connector hubs, round dowel struts, perfectly smooth factory lumber,
a full sphere, garbled text, extra words, watermarks.
```
