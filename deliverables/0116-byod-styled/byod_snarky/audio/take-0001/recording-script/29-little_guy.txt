I think college-aged students would get three times the investment out of a dome system than out of a squandered semester. A realistic five thousand dollar dome home is doable, especially when you rely on the pad for the essentials. The trailer model of the dome world, so to speak. Rich people will find their own way to have what they want without me. The small people, that is who I can impact. Single mothers. The economically vulnerable. Starting families. Bachelors. I wish she had had a stable, economical home system that worked for her and made sense. I would love to be so successful that I can give away domes. Maybe by then I am making dome furniture too.
