## 16. The pad is the floor

Time: 00:00:03.600 - 00:00:04.800

*on screen: No foundation. No slab. Nothing left behind.*

The dome that lands here puts its floor on the pad. So the services have to come up inside the footprint, or they cross the doorway. A utility column if the host wants one: toilet, sink, shower head, outlets, and a meter pedestal at the rim. The dome that arrives does not bring a foundation, because it does not need one. The pad is the floor. The deck it lands on is the deck it lives on. Bringing the carpet is up to the dome owner. I would. I love carpet. Some people want a garage-shop floor through the whole house. I have seen it. It works. When the owner leaves, the pad is exactly as it was. No slab to break out, no footing to dig up, no permit pulled for a structure that will be gone in a year. We already see dome rentals. They are cheaper to build than a house, and most glamping domes are, let us be honest, glorified tents. I do not think their value ever gets realized by renting them out a weekend at a time. But if a person owned their glorified tent and rented the space to put it on instead, now we are talking. Now both sides get something, and both sides are geared for a long-term exchange of service. Renting a house has never been a way forward for me. Buy-here-pay-here anything is for people with no choice but to be used as pawns by the banking system. A dome is the way out. It is my way out, at the very least. If I can bring people along with me, my job here is done.

## 41. Bring your own dome

Time: 00:00:04.800 - 00:00:06.000

*on screen: Keep the value of the home you improve.*

A hotel sells you a night. A short let sells you a fortnight. A lease sells you a year and then charges you to leave early. And a rental home is somebody handing you a money shredder at your convenience, for use on your own stash. One of those sells you the thing most people actually want, which is to live somewhere for a while without handing over the value of everything they do to the place while they are there. Bring your own home. Pay the host for the ground and the services. Improve your house as much as you like, because it is your house, and take every bit of that with you when you go. The source, the model inputs and the experiments should be open. Actual open source, not the kind where the word is doing marketing. The drawings, the prices and the things that fail should all be available to check. Nothing in this film was asserted. Every pad and every dome was drawn by the tool that designs them. Every figure was computed by the model underneath it, and the numbers that argue against this idea are in here with the ones that argue for it. Come and check them.
