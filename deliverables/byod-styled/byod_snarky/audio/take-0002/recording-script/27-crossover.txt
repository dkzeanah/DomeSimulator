So how long does the stay have to be? Run those same four options at every length, and find the month the dome stops being the expensive answer. Nobody should buy a house to stay somewhere for a month. Under that line a short let is the right answer, and we are not going to pretend otherwise. Past it, the gap opens and never closes. Most people fit in the categories this is aimed at: too long to be a guest, too uncertain to sign for a year. That is the part of the market neither a hotel nor a twelve month lease has ever served.
