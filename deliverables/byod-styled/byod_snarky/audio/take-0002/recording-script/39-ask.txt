The goal is a hundred thousand dollars. I think that is a modest number to strive for, and it is modest because the expensive parts are already mine: the land, and somewhere to work. What the money builds is one to three pads on that land, as a test group, and the pad architecture itself made repeatable, the way RV hookups are repeatable, so the second site is a cut list rather than an invention. Most of it goes into modular design and the means to do it. A full woodworking and metalworking shop. CNC plasma torches, three-D printers, a mill, casting materials, a laser, a router: a lab for panelizing the frame and skeleton I already have realized. One to three dome designs that actually suit this, built and measured. Then the web app and its hosting and promotion. Real advertising on every platform I can reach, because I want this idea to spread like the flu rather than sit in a repository. And a camera, because all of it gets published as it is made. What the campaign does not buy is the tracking foundation. A pad that turns to follow the sun is a fine thing to own and I intend to build one, but it is whimsy on the first site, and I am not spending anyone else's money on it.
