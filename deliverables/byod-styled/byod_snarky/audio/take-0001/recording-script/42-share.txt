And one last thing, said plainly. If this campaign does not fund, my plan and my work are the same. I am building this whether it takes one year or ten. What a donation changes is the speed, and whether the drawings reach anybody else on the way. If any of this was worth your time, send it to one person who can carry it further than I can. That is the whole ask. One share from somebody with reach does more for this than a month of me in a field with a chainsaw.
