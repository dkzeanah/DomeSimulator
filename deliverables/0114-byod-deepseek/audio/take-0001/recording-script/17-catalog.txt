Pads come in sizes, and the sizes are not invented. Every design the Dome Creator ships has a foundation diameter, and the tool computes it from its own geometry. Round each one up to the next four feet and you have the pad catalog. Four foot steps, because decking and ring stock come in even lengths, and because the second pad a host builds wants the first one's cut list. Add a bigger dome to the tool, and a bigger pad size appears here on the next run. Nobody edits a list.
