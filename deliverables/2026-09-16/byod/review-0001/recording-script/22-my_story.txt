When I was nineteen, I could borrow five grand against my name for college. I blew that opportunity, joined the military, and now at thirty-two I am back for revenge. Constructive revenge, apparently involving domes. I did not fail because I was stupid. My living situation was unstable. I did not have the support I needed, and I could not focus on what mattered. It cost me tremendously. What would a small home I owned have done for my eighteen-year-old self? I think it would have been revolutionary. That is the person I keep in mind when I design this.
