Pads come in sizes, and so does the hardware. Start with the base model and size it two steps ahead. For my wedge system the plan is to start with a six foot long member, go to ten feet, then twelve: about twelve hundred square feet at that final target size. Any bigger, and we start upping the dome frequency. Sinking capital into the hardware majority is the advantage, because that is the part that remains and gets reused when a bigger dome is wanted. The longer members get collected gradually, on the way. That is why I would put money into a good hardware set: it should remain useful across a planned range of builds.
