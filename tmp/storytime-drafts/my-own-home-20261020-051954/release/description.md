## How it was made

The pictures are the storytime's own 3-D world (website/src/storytime), the dome is the wedge-dome solver's own model, the narration is synthesised (Microsoft Edge TTS) or the owner's own voice, and the lullaby is synthesised. Rendered by sketch_claude storytime at storytime-my-own-home.mp4.
